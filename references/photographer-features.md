# Photographer Feature Reference

Use this reference when the user names a photographer, asks for a master-like look, or needs a distinctive visual direction.

Do not promise exact replication of a living artist's style. Convert photographer references into transferable visual traits and executable editing choices.

## Safe Wording Pattern

Use:
- "Use a visual language inspired by..."
- "Borrow the traits of..."
- "Translate this into..."
- "Aim for..."

Avoid:
- "Exactly imitate..."
- "Make it indistinguishable from..."
- "Copy this living photographer's style..."

## Selection Rules

- Match the photographer direction to the source photo's scene, light, and subject.
- Prefer deceased or historical references when the user does not name a photographer.
- For living artists, describe broad traits and technical choices, not exact replication.
- If the photo lacks the needed ingredients, say so and choose the nearest safe trait. For example, do not force neon-night color onto a plain daylight landscape.

## Henri Cartier-Bresson-Inspired Geometric Street Timing

Best for:
- Street photos with human gesture, geometry, decisive timing, shadows, windows, stairs, crossings.

Visual traits:
- Natural available light.
- Balanced black and white or restrained color.
- Moderate to strong contrast, not crushed.
- Geometry, frame-within-frame, leading lines, decisive subject placement.
- Natural texture and observational mood.

Manual editing direction:
- Crop to strengthen geometry.
- Straighten verticals and horizons.
- Convert to black and white if color distracts from timing.
- Use contrast to clarify subject shape.
- Avoid heavy vignettes or artificial lighting.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Geometric street documentary edit, decisive-moment composition, natural available light, balanced black-and-white tones, subtle grain, preserve candid realism."

## Daido Moriyama-Inspired High-Contrast Grainy Street Black And White

Best for:
- Gritty street photos, night alleys, motion, reflections, imperfect but energetic frames.

Visual traits:
- Harsh, uneven urban light.
- Deep blacks and bright highlights.
- Very high contrast.
- Raw close framing, tilt and imperfection can work.
- Visible grain and rough documentary texture.

Manual editing direction:
- Convert to black and white.
- Increase contrast and blacks.
- Add grain after denoising color speckles.
- Allow some highlight harshness if it serves mood.
- Keep important subject detail readable.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "High-contrast grainy black-and-white street edit, raw urban energy, deep blacks, bright highlights, rough documentary texture, preserve original scene."

## Fan Ho-Inspired Light-Shadow Urban Geometry

Best for:
- Street scenes with strong sun, silhouettes, alleys, stairs, architecture, mist or haze.

Visual traits:
- Strong directional light and shadow.
- Dramatic black and white or very restrained color.
- Geometric composition, negative space, small human figure in large environment.
- Elegant atmospheric depth.

Manual editing direction:
- Emphasize light-shadow separation.
- Keep silhouettes clean.
- Crop for negative space and geometric balance.
- Add dehaze only if it does not destroy atmosphere.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Poetic urban light-and-shadow edit, strong geometric composition, elegant black-and-white tones, small human figure, atmospheric depth, realistic texture."

## Saul Leiter-Inspired Layered Color Street Atmosphere

Best for:
- Street scenes through glass, rain, reflections, foreground color blocks, partial subjects.

Visual traits:
- Soft diffused light through windows, rain, or reflection.
- Gentle contrast.
- Painterly reds/yellows against muted cool backgrounds.
- Layered composition, obstruction, negative space, ambiguity.

Manual editing direction:
- Do not over-sharpen.
- Preserve reflections and foreground layers.
- Reduce global contrast, then selectively guide attention.
- Keep colors rich but not neon.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Layered atmospheric color street edit, soft reflections, muted painterly color, gentle contrast, quiet urban mood, preserve candid realism."

## William Eggleston-Inspired Democratic Everyday Color

Best for:
- Ordinary objects, street corners, cars, diners, signs, domestic scenes, overlooked details.

Visual traits:
- Everyday subject matter treated seriously.
- Strong but believable color.
- Direct composition and democratic attention to the mundane.
- Slight tension between plain scene and vivid palette.

Manual editing direction:
- Keep composition matter-of-fact; avoid excessive cinematic crop.
- Strengthen one or two dominant colors with HSL.
- Keep midtone contrast clear, not glossy.
- Avoid over-cleaning the scene; ordinary details are part of the appeal.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Everyday color documentary edit, vivid but believable local color, direct composition, ordinary details treated with visual importance, realistic texture."

## Stephen Shore-Inspired Calm Color Documentary

Best for:
- Road trips, streets, buildings, intersections, hotel rooms, open daylight scenes.

Visual traits:
- Straightforward color documentary feeling.
- Calm, balanced daylight.
- Accurate perspective and quiet geometry.
- Low drama, strong observation.

Manual editing direction:
- Correct perspective and horizon carefully.
- Keep white balance natural.
- Use moderate contrast and realistic saturation.
- Avoid heavy vignette, fantasy color, or aggressive grain.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Calm color documentary edit, accurate perspective, natural daylight, quiet geometry, restrained saturation, realistic travel observation."

## Joel Meyerowitz-Inspired Warm Street Color And Light

Best for:
- Street life, beach/city scenes, warm daylight, human figures inside open public space.

Visual traits:
- Luminous natural color.
- Human street observation with generous environmental context.
- Warm light, open shadows, gentle complexity.

Manual editing direction:
- Preserve environmental context around people.
- Warm highlights slightly while protecting skin.
- Keep shadows readable.
- Use vibrance lightly to keep color alive.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Warm color street documentary edit, luminous natural light, readable shadows, human figures in environmental context, realistic color."

## Ernst Haas-Inspired Color Motion And Abstraction

Best for:
- Motion blur, reflections, city lights, color fragments, experimental street or travel photos.

Visual traits:
- Expressive color.
- Motion and blur used as design.
- Abstracted shapes and light trails.
- Painterly energy without losing photographic roots.

Manual editing direction:
- Do not automatically remove all motion blur if it is expressive.
- Strengthen color contrast between key shapes.
- Use clarity selectively, not globally.
- Crop to make abstraction intentional.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Expressive color motion edit, painterly photographic blur, vivid light shapes, abstract urban energy, preserve original scene structure."

## Gordon Parks-Inspired Humanist Documentary Portrait

Best for:
- Environmental portraits, documentary people photos, street portraits, social scenes.

Visual traits:
- Human dignity and narrative environment.
- Strong but natural portrait structure.
- Context matters as much as face.
- Black and white or restrained color both work.

Manual editing direction:
- Lift the face enough to read expression.
- Keep background context visible.
- Use gentle dodge and burn to guide attention.
- Avoid beauty-filter smoothing or theatrical over-styling.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Humanist documentary portrait edit, expressive face, meaningful environment, natural light, dignified tonal structure, realistic texture."

## Vivian Maier-Inspired Candid Street Portrait

Best for:
- Candid people, reflections, street portraits, square compositions, everyday gestures.

Visual traits:
- Direct but quiet observation.
- Natural light and real street context.
- Strong central subject or mirrored/reflected subject.
- Often works well in black and white.

Manual editing direction:
- Crop square only if it strengthens the subject.
- Keep face and gesture readable.
- Reduce color distractions or convert to black and white.
- Preserve candid realism.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Candid street portrait edit, natural available light, quiet observational mood, clean subject gesture, subtle black-and-white or restrained color."

## Slim Aarons-Inspired Leisure Color

Best for:
- Pools, resorts, sunny travel, social scenes, lifestyle photos with clean light.

Visual traits:
- Bright affluent leisure mood.
- Clean sunlit color.
- Elegant composition, relaxed people, open space.
- Polished but not heavy.

Manual editing direction:
- Keep whites clean and shadows open.
- Increase vibrance modestly.
- Protect skin and sky.
- Avoid gritty grain or dark cinematic mood.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Sunlit leisure color edit, clean bright palette, relaxed social atmosphere, polished travel-lifestyle feeling, realistic skin and sky."

## Rinko Kawauchi-Inspired Luminous Quiet Detail

Best for:
- Flowers, children, small daily details, soft light, water, glass, quiet still-life moments.

Visual traits:
- Luminous high-key softness.
- Gentle pastel color.
- Shallow attention to small details.
- Quiet, intimate, delicate atmosphere.

Manual editing direction:
- Keep contrast low.
- Lift exposure carefully while protecting highlights.
- Reduce harsh clarity.
- Use local masks to guide attention to the delicate detail.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Luminous quiet detail edit, soft high-key light, gentle pastel color, delicate everyday subject, subtle contrast, realistic photographic texture."

## Alex Webb-Inspired Layered Saturated Street Color

Best for:
- Complex street scenes, strong sun, layered foreground/background, dense color, crowds.

Visual traits:
- Layered composition with multiple planes.
- Saturated but disciplined color.
- Strong light and shadow.
- Complex street energy.

Manual editing direction:
- Crop only enough to clarify layers.
- Protect highlights and deepen shadows.
- Strengthen key color relationships through HSL.
- Avoid flattening the scene into a simple portrait.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Layered saturated street color edit, strong sun and shadow, multiple planes of action, rich disciplined color, realistic documentary detail."

## Gregory Crewdson-Inspired Cinematic Environmental Drama

Best for:
- Empty streets, interiors, suburban night scenes, staged-looking portraits, cinematic spaces.

Visual traits:
- Composed cinematic lighting.
- Cool ambient light with motivated warm practicals.
- Quiet tension, stillness, narrative environment.
- Polished tonal control.

Manual editing direction:
- Shape light with subject/background masks.
- Cool shadows and protect warm lamps.
- Darken corners subtly if it guides the eye.
- Avoid inventing a scene; preserve the original environment.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Cinematic environmental photo edit, cool ambient shadows, warm practical lights, quiet narrative tension, polished realistic lighting, preserve original scene."

## Nan Goldin-Inspired Intimate Flash Diary

Best for:
- Indoor friends, parties, candid social scenes, direct flash, personal documentary moments.

Visual traits:
- Intimate snapshot feeling.
- Direct flash or practical indoor light.
- Saturated reds/yellows, imperfect realism.
- Emotional closeness over polish.

Manual editing direction:
- Preserve snapshot honesty; do not over-retouch.
- Control skin highlights and red casts.
- Keep a little grain/noise if it supports the mood.
- Avoid glamorizing sensitive or private content.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Intimate flash-diary documentary edit, direct indoor light, saturated but realistic color, candid personal mood, preserve natural imperfections."

## Peter Lindbergh-Inspired Restrained Black-And-White Portrait

Best for:
- Natural portraits, expressive faces, simple clothing, window light, mature or textured faces.

Visual traits:
- Soft directional light.
- Refined black and white.
- Moderate contrast with strong facial structure.
- Real skin texture preserved.
- Honest, restrained, intimate mood.

Manual editing direction:
- Convert to black and white after cleaning exposure.
- Preserve pores and facial texture.
- Use dodge and burn for facial structure.
- Avoid beauty smoothing.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Restrained black-and-white natural portrait edit, soft directional light, real skin texture, honest expression, subtle tonal contrast."

## Annie Leibovitz-Inspired Editorial Portrait Lighting

Best for:
- Posed portraits, environmental portraits, dramatic indoor scenes, strong character concepts.

Visual traits:
- Shaped key light and controlled fill.
- Purposeful background.
- Polished cinematic or editorial palette.
- Subject-centered narrative environment.

Manual editing direction:
- Shape light with subject and background masks.
- Darken background to increase narrative focus.
- Keep skin detailed but polished.
- Use color grading to unify environment and subject.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Editorial environmental portrait edit, shaped key light, controlled background, polished cinematic color, strong subject presence, realistic skin texture."

## Steve McCurry-Inspired Dense Travel Color Portraiture

Best for:
- Travel portraits, markets, cultural scenes, expressive eyes, colorful backgrounds.

Visual traits:
- Clear natural or soft directional light.
- Rich midtones, controlled highlights.
- Dense reds, blues, yellows, and earthy tones.
- Close expression and strong sense of place.

Manual editing direction:
- Protect skin tone before boosting color.
- Increase vibrance more than saturation.
- Strengthen key local colors through HSL.
- Darken background slightly to hold attention on face.
- Add clarity to eyes and fabric, not skin.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Dense travel color portrait edit, expressive eyes, rich natural colors, controlled highlights, strong sense of place, realistic skin tone."

## Sebastião Salgado-Inspired Epic Black-And-White Documentary

Best for:
- Landscapes, labor scenes, documentary travel, dramatic weather, large-scale human/environment scenes.

Visual traits:
- Sweeping black-and-white tonal drama.
- Deep shadows, luminous highlights, strong texture.
- Epic scale and environmental gravity.

Manual editing direction:
- Convert to black and white.
- Use sky/subject masks to build tonal depth.
- Add clarity and texture carefully.
- Avoid crushing important shadow detail.

AI prompt-safe wording:
- "Epic black-and-white documentary edit, dramatic tonal range, detailed texture, luminous highlights, deep readable shadows, realistic environmental scale."

## Additional Trait Combinations

Use these when no named photographer is needed:

High-contrast black-and-white documentary:
- Strong tonal separation, visible texture, subject emotion, minimal color dependency.

Natural-light restrained portrait:
- Clean skin tone, soft directional light, low to medium contrast, honest texture.

Geometric street composition:
- Straightened lines, clear subject placement, cropped distractions, controlled contrast.

Dense color travel documentary:
- Rich local colors, strong midtone contrast, protected highlights, believable skin and sky.

Quiet low-saturation editorial portrait:
- Muted palette, clean background, subtle skin polish, controlled shadows, calm mood.

Layered color street:
- Foreground obstruction, reflections, separated planes, disciplined saturated color, strong light.

Blue-hour cinematic architecture:
- Cool ambient base, warm lamps, clean verticals, controlled reflections, no fake sky.
